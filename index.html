How sponsorship works

From your gift to a first breath

  1. i.

    A family asks

    Their midwife tells us about a family who can’t cover the cost.

  2. ii.

    You give

    Your gift is matched to that family’s care.

  3. iii.

    We pay the midwife

    Money goes to the midwife or supplier, never as cash.

  4. iv.

    A baby is born

    With the family’s blessing, we send you their news.
