Our approach

We raise funds to help U.S.-based families afford home births attended by qualified midwives.

Sponsorship

One family, one birth, one gift at a time.

A midwife tells us about a family who needs help. We check the details with her, and once the money is raised we pay her directly.

You can cover a birth kit ($100), partially fund a birth ($1,000), or cover the full course of care from first visit to six weeks after the birth ($6,000). Smaller gifts are pooled until a family’s care is paid for.

Legal campaign

We support families with home birth costs today and champion affordable home birth care for tomorrow.
